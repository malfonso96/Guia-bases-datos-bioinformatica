# Pubmed

🔗**Website:** [Pubmed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/)

## 📌 What is it used for?

PubMed is a free bibliographic database for biomedical and life sciences literature, maintained by the National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI). It allows researchers to search for scientific articles, reviews, clinical studies and other publications related to medicine, biology, genetics, biotechnology and related fields.

It is commonly used to find scientific evidence, explore research topics, identify relevant publications and retrieve references for literature reviews and scientific research.

## 🗂️ Type of database

| **By data origyn** | **By data review** |
|---|---|
| Primary | Reviewed |

PubMed applies selection and indexing criteria to its bibliographic records; however, this does not mean that NCBI conducts peer review of each individual article.


## ⭐ Key features

- Contains more than 39 million citations and abstracts from biomedical and life sciences literature.

- MeSH terms allow users to search for articles using standardised subject headings rather than relying only on keywords.

- Advanced Search Builder allows users to combine search terms and apply filters using Boolean operators (AND, OR, NOT).

- Clinical Queries provides specialised search filters for clinical studies, diagnosis, therapy and other evidence-based medicine topics.

- Each publication has a unique PMID (PubMed Identifier), which allows it to be identified and retrieved.

- Links to full-text articles when available, including publications hosted in PubMed Central (PMC).

- Results can be exported in formats such as CSV, XML and MEDLINE, and retrieved programmatically using the NCBI E-utilities API.
