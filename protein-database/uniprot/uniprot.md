# 🧪 UniProtKB – UniProt Knowledgebase

🔗 **Website:** https://www.uniprot.org/

## 📌 What is it used for?
UniProtKB is the main reference database for **protein information**. For each protein it collects the amino acid sequence together with its function, subcellular location, post-translational modifications, domains, interactions and links to other databases. It is commonly used to look up what a protein does and to download its sequence in FASTA format.

## 🗂️ Type of database
| By data origin | By data review |
|---|---|
| Secondary | Reviewed (Swiss-Prot) |

Maintained by the **UniProt Consortium**: EMBL-EBI, the Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics (SIB) and the Protein Information Resource (PIR).

## ⭐ Key features
- It is divided into two sections:
  - **Swiss-Prot**: manually annotated and reviewed by expert curators (*Reviewed*)
  - **TrEMBL**: automatically annotated, not reviewed (*Unreviewed*)
- Each entry has a unique accession number (e.g. **P05362** for human ICAM1).
- Every annotation shows its **evidence**: experimental or predicted.
- Its origins go back to **PIR**, which grew out of Margaret Dayhoff's *Atlas of Protein Sequence and Structure* (1965), the first biological database.
